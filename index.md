# Finger Soccer 3D online 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-10-03

## 中文

**收集的信息**
- 设备标识：用于创建匿名游客账号，保存你的进度。
- Apple 登录信息：仅在你选择用 Apple 登录时收集，用于把进度绑定到你的 Apple ID。
- 游戏进度：金币、钻石、胜场、已购买的球队和阵型等，用于保存和恢复进度。
- 在线对战数据：昵称和 IP 地址，用于匹配对手和联机。

**第三方服务**：Microsoft PlayFab（存储账号和进度）、Photon（联机）、Apple（登录和内购）。支付由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到你的支付信息。

**我们不会**出售你的信息，也不会跨应用或网站追踪你。游戏目前没有广告。

**删除数据**：在主菜单点两次 Delete Account，即可删除云端进度、解除 Apple 绑定并清空本地数据。如需彻底删除账号记录，请发邮件给我们。

**儿童**：游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

## English

**Information we collect**
- Device identifier: creates an anonymous guest account to keep your progress.
- Sign in with Apple data: only if you choose to sign in with Apple, to link your progress to your Apple ID.
- Game progress: coins, gems, wins, purchased teams and formations, to save and restore your progress.
- Online match data: nickname and IP address, to find opponents and connect.

**Third-party services**: Microsoft PlayFab (account and progress storage), Photon (multiplayer), Apple (sign-in and purchases). Apple processes payments; we never receive your payment details.

**We do not** sell your information or track you across other apps or websites. The game currently shows no ads.

**Deleting your data**: tap Delete Account twice in the main menu to erase your cloud progress, unlink Apple, and clear local data. To remove your whole account record, email us.

**Children**: the game is not intended for children under 13.
